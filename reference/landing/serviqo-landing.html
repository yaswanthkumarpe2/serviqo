AI support

Two distinct jobs. Never confused.

Serviqo AI can talk to customers directly for approved, low-risk work — and it separately assists your agents behind the scenes. The two never blur together.

Autonomous AI support

When an organization enables it, Serviqo AI replies to customers directly for greetings, FAQs, knowledge-base questions, basic troubleshooting, order status, and collecting information — always labeled, never disguised as a person.

  • ✓Every AI reply carries the Serviqo AI label — the customer always knows who they're talking to
  • ✓Identifies intent, recommends articles, creates and categorizes tickets, routes to the right department
  • ✓Hands off the moment a rule requires a person — with a private summary so the customer never repeats themselves

Human agent AI assistance

Inside the agent workspace, AI drafts and suggests — it never sends. Every suggestion is reviewed, edited or discarded by the person handling the conversation.

  • ✓Suggested replies, thread summaries, sentiment, and detected intent
  • ✓Suggested category, priority and department — applied by a human, recorded in the ticket history
  • ✓Relevant knowledge articles and a recommended next action, surfaced next to the reply box

Where autonomy stops. AI cannot issue refunds, change passwords, delete accounts, access payment credentials, or close a critical ticket — for either capability. Consequential actions always require a person.

Ticketing and SLA

A clock on every promise you make.

Any conversation can become a ticket with a number, an owner and a deadline. Response and resolution targets are set per priority, and agents are warned before the timer runs out.

  • ✓Nine states, one lifecycle. Open through reopened, with the full activity history attached.
  • ✓Departments and skills. Billing, refunds, technical, security — agents belong to the teams they know.
  • ✓AI-suggested category and priority, applied by a human and logged as such.
Automation — separate from AI

Deterministic messages don't need a model.

Welcome messages, business-hours replies, queue position, ticket-created confirmations and SLA notifications run on Serviqo's rules engine — instant, predictable, and consuming zero AI calls.

  • ✓Rules read as sentences — when / if / then — not a node canvas nobody wants to debug at 2am
  • ✓Dry-run preview shows exactly what a rule would have matched before you turn it on
  • ✓Runs whether or not AI is enabled for the organization

Security and reliability

Built for more than one company.

Every business record carries an organization ID, and isolation is enforced in the data layer. These are the principles the platform is being built to — not an independent audit or certification claim.

Tenant isolation

Queries are scoped to the caller's organization at the repository layer.

Rotating tokens

Short-lived access tokens with revocable refresh tokens. Passwords hashed, never logged.

Roles that mean something

Checked on every route and every socket event, not just hidden in the UI.

Audit trail

Every assignment, status change and AI-applied suggestion is logged with an actor.

Uploads under control

Type, extension and size validated server-side; files stored under generated names.

Your data, your retention

Configure retention, export a customer's history, delete it on request.
